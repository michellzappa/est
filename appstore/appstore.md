### description

EST is a free card game about patterns. The deck has 81 cards, and every card combines count, color, shape, and fill.

Find three cards where each trait is either the same on all three cards or different on all three cards. If one trait has a two-and-one split, the cards do not make a set.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

• Solo 81: clear the full deck against the clock. An eligible personal best can go to Game Center.
• Quick 27: play the 27 solid cards for a shorter round. The fill trait stays fixed.
• Duel: race another player on one phone or across two devices with Game Center matchmaking. On iPad, four players can share one table.

LEARN THE RULE

The guided tutorial teaches the four traits with a worked set, a non-set, and a practice board. The rules screen stays close when you need a reminder.

EXPLORE THE MATH

Every card is a point in a four-dimensional space, and every set is a line. Build cards from four base-3 coordinates, see the card that completes any pair, and explore the geometry of the deck.

CUSTOMIZE THE LOOK

Choose a color theme and fill style. Turn sound effects and haptics on or off. EST has no ads or subscriptions. An optional one-time support purchase helps pay for maintenance, adds a permanent supporter mark and optional cosmetic finish, and provides early TestFlight access when available. No gameplay is locked.

EST is open source and inspired by SET, the classic card game created by Marsha Falco. EST uses its own name, artwork, and code. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by SET's makers.

Made for iPhone and iPad by Michell Zappa at Centaur Labs.
