EST can be played without an account. On first launch, tap Skip if the
tutorial appears. App Review can test Solo 81, Quick 27, and one-phone Duel
without signing in. Game Center is optional and is used for leaderboards and
two-device matchmaking.

The app has no ads or subscriptions. It includes one optional, one-time
non-consumable support purchase. It unlocks no gameplay: the supporter mark
and optional cosmetic finish are purely cosmetic, and every game mode and
feature is available without payment. App Review can test the app without
buying it. The purchase is available from Settings > Support EST and can be
tested with the included StoreKit configuration.

The app does not collect personal data. Optional anonymous diagnostics can be
disabled in Settings; when enabled, they send one coarse aggregate batch per
week and never include gameplay events or identifiers. The source code and
privacy policy are available at:
https://github.com/michellzappa/est
